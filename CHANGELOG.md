# Patch notes

Newest first. Every pull request adds a line under **Unreleased**. When Sam or Niels publishes a session update, Unreleased becomes that session's heading.

Tag each line: **New**, **Changed**, **Fixed** or **Lore**. Add the author in brackets.

## Unreleased

- **New** The world has air now. A thin glowing atmosphere wraps the globe: blue on the day side, amber where the sun is setting, and a bright ring when the sun stands behind it. Land near sunset turns golden, and lava, town lamps and the burning edge of new ground glow softly into the dark. Zoom in, or lie the map flat on the table, and the top and bottom of the view drift out of focus, so the world looks like a miniature on the table. Phones get a lighter version, and a slow device drops it by itself. (Sam)
- **Changed** Zoom in and the world fills in: fields and hedgerows round Valarius and Orda, rivers that widen towards the sea with small streams joining them, clumped tree crowns in the woods and loose stone under the steepest slopes. Weather is now a few large, soft clouds lit on their sunny side, and their shadows drift over the land. Trees only sway and the shore only ripples when you are close, so the overview stays calm, and the hand-painted look on computers eases off as you zoom so the fine detail stays sharp. (Sam)
- **Changed** Quieter motion, so the map is easier to read. Ash from newly decoded ground drifts up more gently and settles. Lightning comes rarer and softer, as one glow in the clouds instead of a strobe. Fireflies only show once you zoom in, embers flicker less, and the brass rings are fainter and turn slower. The dot on Recap pulses three times and then stays still. Hans's and the black box's lines all stay up long enough to read (about 200 words a minute), the black box glitches less often, and the small round buttons on cards are bigger to tap. (Sam)
- **Changed** A calmer, tidier map. Place names make room for each other: when two would touch, one moves above its pin or fades out until you zoom in, and no name hides under the title plate, the bottom bar or an open card any more. The six little buttons bottom left are now one tools button that opens a short list: hand-painted map and sound first, then the silly modes, each with its name and on or off. Hans only breathes while he waits and comes alive when he talks or decodes. (Sam)
- **Fixed** "Missed something?" no longer runs into the edge of the bottom bar on computers, and the character picker on Characters no longer lets the section headings show through behind it. (Sam)
- **Changed** The world looks like an old engraved atlas now: hills and mountains catch the light from the north-west and cast soft violet shadows, steep slopes are drawn in fine ink strokes that appear as you zoom in, and snowy peaks have blue shade. Coasts get one clean ink line with sandy beaches or bare cliffs, pale shallows and deeper bands of sea, pack ice in the far north, and the sun glints on the water. Plains and woods are soft watercolour washes. The globe also draws a little faster. (Sam)
- **New** The Wind Vault's flying city now casts its shadow on the land below. It swings round with the sun, stretches long at dawn and dusk, and is gone at night. (Sam)
- **Changed** On computers the globe looks hand-painted: colour lies in soft brush strokes on grainy paper, while coasts, rivers and the route stay crisp ink. A brush button bottom left switches back to the plain map. Phones keep the plain map, and a slow computer drops the paint by itself. (Sam)
- **Changed** The recap opens like a chapter in Hans's book: chapter number, the session's name and its line from the Chronicle, then the stops. Hans's lines start with a brass drop cap. (Sam)
- **Changed** Tapping a place on the map unfolds it: the pin's name flies up into the card's title. Phones and browsers without this effect simply open the card. (Sam)
- **New** Fly the route: on a replay card ("Fly it") or at the end of the recap ("Fly the route"), the camera follows that session's road in letterbox while the ink draws it and the party walks along. Tap anywhere or Skip to stop. (Sam)
- **New** Roll from your sheet: on Characters, tap a skill, an ability, a save or an attack and real 3D dice tumble across the screen. Attacks roll to hit and damage together (a natural 20 doubles the damage dice), and Hans comments on the very good and very bad rolls. Handy if your dice are under the table. (Sam)
- **New** Living sky: a slow day and night passes over the globe (one day every eight minutes). After dark the towns the crew knows light their lamps, thin weather drifts over the ground you have seen, and now and then lightning flashes inside a cloud, with thunder if sound is on. (Sam)
- **New** Sound of the world: a speaker button bottom left. Wind over the frozen north, fire and crackle near the Fire Vault and the Fire Sea, birds in Summerwood, a deep rumble near the Dead Valley, and a faint beat leaking from Hans's headphones. It follows where you look, and new ground crackles as it burns open. Off until you turn it on. (Sam)
- **New** Hans reads aloud: a speaker button on the recap and replay cards. Hans speaks in your phone's own voice, pitched down; the black box goes lower still. It stays on for the next card until you turn it off. (Sam)
- **New** Postcard from Hans: in the Chronicle, one tap turns the map as it is on your screen into a framed postcard with a line from Hans, ready to send to the group chat or save. (Sam)
- **New** Table mode keeps your screen from dimming while the phone or tablet lies on the table. (Sam)
- **Changed** When new ground is decoded, the old chart burns away: a flickering ember line crosses it, the paper behind it is briefly scorched, and ash drifts up and cools to grey. (Sam)
- **New** On the new Netlify address, players Sam invites can sign in (email and password, or Google). Signed in, you pick your character once, and "Missed something?" goes straight to Sam instead of through WhatsApp; the storyline vote and shared pins work for the whole table, on every device. Sam gets the DM view. Without signing in, nothing changes. (Niels)
- **New** Replay the journey: open the Chronicle and tap a session, or "Replay the whole journey", and Hans rewinds the chart to how it stood after that session. The cloud closes back in, later places disappear, and then the session plays forward: new ground clears, the route draws itself in ink and the party walks it. Step back and forth with the arrows, or tap "Back to now". (Sam)
- **Changed** In the recap, the session's route now draws itself as the fog lifts, with the party walking along it, instead of popping in at once. (Sam)
- **Changed** The assassin behind the spider button is a woman now: blonde hair under the hood, a long braid that flies as she leaps. (Niels)
- **New** A fourth silly button, with a spider on it. Press it and see what drops from the ceiling. (Niels)
- **Changed** Places has a short, fixed set of tags: tavern, shop, healing, work, travel, danger, and the factions (Blood Guild, Order of the Seal, Order of the Air, rebels, elves, One-Eyed Champions). Everyday words still work in search: "food" or "inn" finds the taverns, "harbour" finds travel points. When you edit a place's tags, the set is offered as one-tap buttons. (Niels)
- **Lore** The small southern continent has a name: Fraas-Den, a land of free city-states, small baronies, kingdoms and merchant-republics, and the place Stig was born and calls home. Grisshaven is a small harbour on its coast, in Myratma, the Patrechon Province ruled by a Prince-elect, where Stig grew up. (Niels)
- **Lore** A small continent has appeared in the southern sea, still almost all cloud. Only a rumour has come through: the town of Grisshaven, and in it a tavern called the Lighthouse. (Niels)
- **New** A known-bugs list at the top of `BACKLOG.md`. Spot something broken? Tell Sam or Niels and it goes on the list. (Niels)
- **New** A shared backlog in `BACKLOG.md`: the list of what Sam and Niels plan to build next. Every working session now starts from it. (Niels)
- **Changed** Character sheets read like a classic D&D sheet: Armor Class, Initiative and Speed up top, then hit points, then the six abilities with big modifiers, their scores and saving throws side by side (a teal dot marks a proficient save), plus Proficiency and Passive Perception. On the smallest phones the abilities fold into two rows of three. (Niels)
- **Changed** The Sheets button in the dock is now called Characters. On small phones the dock labels sit a little tighter so every name fits. (Niels)
- **New** Places: a small edit button on every place lets you add or remove its tags. Your tags stay on your own device, marked "amended", and "Back to Hans's tags" undoes them. (Niels)
- **New** Places tab in the dock, next to Sheets: every place on the map in one searchable list. Each place has a type (town, tavern, castle, vault, cave, natural wonder and more) and tags, so searching "food" finds the taverns. Filter by type, tap a tag to see everything with it, and taverns and prisons inside cities (the Wet Owl, the Crow's Nest) now have their own entries. (Niels)
- **Changed** The site moved to https://pompelmoes-garandor.github.io/chronicle-of-valeria/ now that Sam and Niels run it together. Save the new link; the old one stops working. (Sam)
- **New** Site source in `source/`, co-working rules in `CLAUDE.md`, a PR checklist and these patch notes. (Sam)
